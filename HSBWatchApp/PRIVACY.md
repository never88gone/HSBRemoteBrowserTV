## 🇨🇳 中文版 (Chinese Version)

《糖葫芦智能遥控器》（以下简称“本应用”）是一款致力于为用户提供极致大屏控制与多模协同体验的智能遥控工具。我们深知个人隐私对您的重要性，并严格遵循苹果公司的 App Store 审核准则（特别是 Guideline 5.1 数据收集与存储）及相关法律法规。

### 1. 核心原则：零数据收集
- **本应用不收集任何个人身份信息（PII）**。
- 我们不记录、不追踪、不上报您的设备唯一标识符（如 IDFA、IDFV 等）。
- 本应用无需注册账号，无需登录，没有任何云端数据库收集用户数据。

### 2. 权限使用说明
本应用在运行期间可能申请以下系统权限，所有权限仅用于实现核心本地控制功能，绝不会将任何数据上传至第三方服务器：
- **本地网络权限 (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription)**：
  仅用于在局域网（Wi-Fi）内自动通过 Bonjour 广播协议（如 `_airplay._tcp`, `_mediaremotetv._tcp`, `_companion-link._tcp` 等）发现并连接您的 Apple TV、Mac 电脑或糖葫芦大屏浏览器，所有遥控指令和按键事件均为端到端局域网传输，完全离线运行。
- **相机权限 (NSCameraUsageDescription)**：
  仅用于扫描 Apple TV 或智能大屏上显示的配对二维码以建立安全握手连接。相机画面仅在本地设备内存中实时处理，绝不拍摄、保存或上传任何图像数据。
- **运动与健身权限 (NSMotionUsageDescription)**：
  仅用于体感空中飞鼠光标控制与抬手/翻腕手势判定，运动传感器数据仅在设备端侧实时计算，不进行任何持久化记录。
- **语音识别权限 (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription)**：
  仅用于支持用户在输入抽屉中通过系统内置语音听写直接向大屏发送文字，所有语音处理均由 iOS 系统原生框架执行。

### 3. 数据安全与第三方共享
- **零第三方 SDK 追踪**：本应用不包含任何第三方广告 SDK、用户行为分析 SDK 或社交分享追踪工具。
- **零数据分享**：我们不会向任何商业机构、广告商或数据中介出售、出租或分享任何信息。

## 🇺🇸 English Version

**Tanghulu Smart Remote** ("the App") is designed to provide high-quality, ultra-low-latency remote control for Apple TV, Mac, and large screen displays. We respect and protect your privacy and strictly adhere to Apple's App Store Review Guidelines (specifically Guideline 5.1: Data Collection and Storage).

### 1. Core Principle: Zero Data Collection
- **The App does NOT collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**.
- We do not record, track, or harvest device identifiers such as IDFA or IDFV.
- No user account or cloud registration is required. All features work immediately out-of-the-box.

### 2. Device Permissions & Purpose
The App may request the following iOS permissions strictly for local on-device operation. No data is transmitted to external servers:
- **Local Network Access (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription)**:
  Used exclusively to discover and connect to your Apple TV, Mac, or smart screen displays within the local Wi-Fi network via Bonjour protocol. All HID commands, mouse trackpad events, and navigation keystrokes are transmitted strictly point-to-point within your local network.
- **Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription)**:
  Used solely for scanning pairing QR codes displayed on your Apple TV or screen. Frames are processed live in device memory; no photos are saved or uploaded.
- **Motion & Sensor Data (NSMotionUsageDescription)**:
  Used exclusively for spatial air-mouse cursor interaction and wrist-gesture navigation. Sensor calculations remain transient on-device.
- **Speech Recognition (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription)**:
  Used optionally for voice-to-text dictation to transmit text search queries to the television.

### 3. Third-Party Sharing & Tracking
- **No Third-Party SDKs**: The App does NOT integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or user tracking services.
- **No Data Disclosure**: We do not sell, rent, or disclose any user data to third parties under any circumstances.
