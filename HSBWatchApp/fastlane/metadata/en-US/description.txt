Tanghulu Smart Remote is a versatile, ultra-responsive companion controller designed for Apple TV, Mac, and smart screen displays. Featuring refined modern aesthetics and zero-latency local protocols, it transforms your iPhone and Apple Watch into powerful, intuitive input devices.

[Core Interactive Scenarios]

1. Native macOS AirPlay Media & Volume Center:
• 100% Clientless & Zero-Install: Communicates natively via macOS built-in AirPlay Receiver (Port 7000) with 0 software to install, 0 background daemons, and 0 accessibility permissions needed on the Mac;
• Global Hardware Volume Control: Volume stepping up/down, smooth volume slider indication, and one-tap hardware mute toggle;
• Full Playback Management: System-wide Play/Pause, 10s Fast-Forward/Rewind, and Next/Previous track switching;
• Vinyl Record Visual Card: Elegant vinyl aesthetic accompanied by live dynamic waveform frequency visualizer;
• Instant Native Handshake: Low-level TCP health check for real-time connection status with clear native setup guidance.

2. Apple TV System Remote:
• Ergonomic D-pad directional navigation wheel with tactile haptic feedback;
• Integrated Control Island: Quick seek 10s backward/forward, play/pause, Home, Menu, Mute, Standby/Sleep, and volume controls;
• Anti-Double-Firing verification to prevent duplicate key taps; supports Wake-on-LAN (WoL) and auto-reconnection;
• Streamlined software keyboard entry for effortless large-screen typing and search.

3. Standalone Apple Watch Companion App:
• Native D-Pad Haptic Wheel: Ergonomically crafted directional wheel tailored for watchOS;
• Digital Crown Infinite Scroll: Rotate the crown for pixel-perfect smooth scrolling through web pages and documents;
• Wearable Air Mouse: Utilizes 6-axis motion sensors for intuitive wrist-guided air pointing and gestures;
• Standalone Media Control: Independently adjust volume and control playback directly from your wrist.

4. IPTV & Television Channel Tuning:
• Dual-tower CH+/- and VOL+/- stepping controls for classic TV watching;
• Instant-play shortcuts for favorite TV channels;
• Numeric keypad channel dialing and convenient EPG guide access.

5. PDF Presentation & Live Drawing Whiteboard:
• Large slide turners and first/last page jumping for confident presentation control;
• On-screen laser pointer and real-time live drawing whiteboard with multi-color palette and undo support.

6. Large Screen Browser Touchpad & Edge AI Assistant:
• High-precision dampening touchpad and floating cursor for smart TV browsers;
• On-device MLX local LLM assistant offering 100% private, offline TV guidance and Q&A.

[Seamless Local Connectivity]
• Instant Bonjour zero-configuration discovery over local Wi-Fi;
• Fully functional offline demo mode allows testing all controls and haptics without external hardware;
• Privacy-first architecture: No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, zero data collection.
