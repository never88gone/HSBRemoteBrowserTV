Tanghulu Smart Remote is a versatile, ultra-responsive companion controller designed for Apple TV, Mac, and smart screen displays. Featuring refined modern aesthetics and zero-latency local protocols, it transforms your iPhone and Apple Watch into powerful, intuitive input devices.

[Core Interactive Scenarios]

1. Native Mac System Control & Wireless Trackpad:
• Precision Wireless Trackpad: Supports single-finger smooth cursor gliding, single-tap left click, and two-finger tap for right-click context menus;
• Ergonomic Ballistics Curve: Pixel-accurate slow micro-adjustments and smooth acceleration for effortless cross-screen flicks;
• Smooth Two-Finger Scrolling: Infinite continuous scrolling with natural physics inertia for documents and web pages;
• Fullscreen Trackpad Mode: One-tap toggle to expand the trackpad across the entire viewport for maximum control area;
• Native Backspace & Typing: Full hardware Backspace/Delete key integration alongside streamlined text dispatch;
• Complete System Controls: Global volume HUD adjustments, one-tap mute, Apple Music media playback, and Mission Control triggering.

2. Apple TV System Remote:
• Ergonomic D-pad directional navigation wheel with tactile haptic feedback;
• Integrated Control Island: Quick seek 10s backward/forward, play/pause, Home, Menu, Mute, Standby, and volume controls;
• Streamlined software keyboard entry for effortless large-screen typing and search.

3. IPTV & Television Channel Tuning:
• Dual-tower CH+/- and VOL+/- stepping controls for classic TV watching;
• Instant-play shortcuts for favorite TV channels;
• Numeric keypad channel dialing and convenient EPG guide access.

4. PDF Presentation & Live Drawing Whiteboard:
• Large slide turners and first/last page jumping for confident presentation control;
• On-screen laser pointer and real-time live drawing whiteboard with multi-color palette and undo support.

5. Apple Watch Motion & Smart Assistant:
• Wearable motion sync engine: wrist flip to turn slides, shake to refresh;
• On-device private local LLM assistant for offline TV control guidance and Q&A.

[Seamless Local Connectivity]
• Instant Bonjour zero-configuration discovery over local Wi-Fi;
• Fully functional offline demo mode allows testing all controls and haptics without external hardware;
• Privacy-first architecture: No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, zero data collection.
