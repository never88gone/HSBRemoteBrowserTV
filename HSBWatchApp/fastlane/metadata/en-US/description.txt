Tanghulu Smart Remote is a versatile companion controller designed for Apple TV, Mac, and large screen displays. Featuring refined aesthetics and ultra-low latency protocols, it transforms your iPhone and Apple Watch into powerful input devices.

[Core Interactive Scenarios]
1. System Remote:
• 270pt tactile D-pad wheel with precision directional navigation and haptic feedback;
• Integrated Control Island: media seek, play/pause, Home, Back, Mute, Standby, and volume controls;
• Streamlined software keyboard entry for effortless large-screen typing.

2. IPTV & Media Playback Control:
• Dual-tower CH+/- and VOL+/- stepping controls;
• Quick access to popular channels with instant channel switching;
• Numeric keypad selection and EPG guide access.

3. PDF Presentation & Live Drawing:
• Big presentation slide turners and first/last page jumping;
• On-screen laser pointer and drawing whiteboard with color picker and undo support.

4. Apple Watch Motion & Smart Assistant:
• Wearable motion sync engine: wrist flip to turn slides, shake to refresh;
• On-device private local LLM assistant for offline TV control guidance.

[Seamless Connectivity]
• Zero-config automatic Bonjour discovery on local Wi-Fi networks;
• Direct IP pairing support;
• Built-in offline exploration mode for immediate feature preview anywhere.
